Connecting your glasses

  1. Open Tide on your glasses and on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Connect both devices to the same trusted Wi-Fi network.
  3. In Tide, open Settings → Pair glasses, then select the discovered device. If it does not appear, enter its local IP address and port 8888 manually.

Keep Tide open on the glasses while connecting. Local network access must be allowed on your iPhone or iPad.

Routines and AI

Browse or create routines in Routines. Add a routine to the glasses queue and select it on the glasses when you are ready. AI step generation requires connected glasses, an internet connection and your permission to share data with DeepSeek.

To withdraw AI data permission, open Settings → AI privacy. Local routines remain available.
